## 무슨 삐라인가

Original vintage political propaganda poster titled U. S., HANDS OFF SOUTH KOREA!, published by the International Union of Students (IUS). This striking and historically significant poster features a bold graphic of a US military police officer MP in a helmet, depicted in a minimalist, almost skeletal style. He is shown walking past a red-and-white barrier with a large, circular STOP sign, symbolising the demand to halt American military presence in the region. The background consists of a bright green map of the Korean peninsula set against a solid light-blue sky. The composition is framed by powerful typography, with the title in bold black at the top. At the bottom, the dates 1950.6.25 and 1963.6.25 mark the 13th anniversary of the start of the Korean War, emphasising a long-standing call for solidarity and an end to foreign intervention. Produced by the IUS, an organisation that was central to coordinating global student support for anti-imperialist movements, this piece is an excellent example of international political graphic design from the early 1960s. Fair condition, folds, creasing, tears, pinholes, tape on edges, small paper losses, paper skimming. Country of issue: Czechoslovakia, designer: Unknown, size (cm): 40.5x29, year of printing: 1963.
