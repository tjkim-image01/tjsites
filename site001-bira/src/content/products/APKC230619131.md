## 무슨 삐라인가

A Korean War propaganda leaflet published by the United States. One side of the leaflet contains a proclamation from General Douglas MacArthur (1880-1964) about how the North Koreans will be subject to principles accepted by civilized countries during the military conflict. On verso is a picture of MacArthur with a message to listen to the News broadcast from the headquarters of MacArthur every night at 9 o'clock. The leaflets were dropped onto enemy troops. Foxing. 4 x 6.25 inches.
