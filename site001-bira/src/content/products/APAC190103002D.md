## 무슨 삐라인가

Description: Lot of Korean War UN propaganda flyers. The flyers are attached to official documentation that lists in English the name, language, designation, target, remarks, art work description and full translation of text. Confidential booklet dated "28 MAY 1945" and titled "SUICIDE WEAPONS AND TACTICS KNOW YOUR ENEMY!" with text at bottom right reading "CinCPac-CinCPOA BULLETIN 126-45. All in good to very good condition. G3.
