## 무슨 삐라인가

Lot of two American propaganda leaflets dropped on Japanese soldiers during WWII encouraging Japanese soldiers to surrender. The leaflets state that Japanese soldiers will be treated well if they surrender. One leaflet reads, 'Attention American soldiers - This leaflet guarantees humane treatment to any Japanese desiring to cease resistance. Take him immediately to your nearest commissioned officer. By order C.G. US Forces.' The other leaflet states directions in English and Japanese on how to surrender to American troops abroad, '1. Come slowly toward the American lines with your hands raised high above your head carrying only this card.', approx. 11.75 x 8.5 inches. Mailing folds.
