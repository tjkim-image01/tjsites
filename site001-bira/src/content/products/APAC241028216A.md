## 무슨 삐라인가

Rare original pen and ink art   for a propaganda leaflet that was created to be dropped on Chinese and North Korean troops. The artwork features a giant United Nations officer standing in front of a United Nations flag and looking down at two obviously frightened Chinese officers who cling to his arm as they look across a chasm where the Grim Reaper, in Pied Piper of Hamelin fashion, is leading Chinese troops over the edge where the skeletons of their comrades await them. A row of cannon barrels flank the U.N. officer.   The propaganda leaflet was copied from this pen & ink drawing. Chinese characters were added to the officer's sleeve. The back of the paper leaflet greets the Chinese troops, "We are the United Nations troops in North Korea, different countries. We welcome you!!.. ....and then goes on to proclaim that the U.N.troops hold no grudges against the Chinese officers and men but have to treat them as enemies and fight them ....."the saying goes: Treat a friend like a friend! As treat an enemy like an enemy! . This drawing has not been removed from the frame. The visible part measures "16.25" x 10.25" and is housed in a 24.5" x 18.5 frame.
