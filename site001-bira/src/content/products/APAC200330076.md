## 무슨 삐라인가

WW2 Japan. 1945 Australian post Japanese surrender propaganda air drop leaflet in Japanese & English intended to give Japanese soldiers safe passage to allied soldiers to surrender, "The Japanese with this message has ceased resistance. He should be treated well in accordance with international law. Take him to the nearest Commanding Officer. C-in-C Allied Forces. Reverse states (in Japanese) Japan has surrendered - The war has ended - Peace has come." Size 17 x 15cm.
