## 무슨 삐라인가

Six Korean War propaganda leaflets, including 'Is this the kind of life you want? The Communist Party allows you to live like this,' no. 8589, 'North Korea today,' no. 8588, 'Come back, kid,' no. 8586, 'The devil's hand that blocks the way to life,' no. 8561, 'It's safe now! Soldier Liu Jicheng, 538th Regiment, 8th Division, CPC,' no. R.8555, and 'These armed Communist pigeons brought suffering and death to the North Korean people,' no. 8233.
