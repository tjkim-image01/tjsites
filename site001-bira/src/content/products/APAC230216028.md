## 무슨 삐라인가

Korean War propaganda flyer dropped over American and British soldiers during the Korean War encouraging troops to surrender and end the war, 8 x 5.25 inches. Graphic on obverse shows a beautiful woman waking up in the morning singing, 'Oh! What a Beautiful Morning!'. Verso reads, 'It's a wonderful little tune. People like to sing it or whistle it at 11 o'clock in the morning...Think of your family...Every morning is beautiful for them. But what about you? How many more beautiful mornings will you see, if any?' Minor soiling on flyer.
