## 무슨 삐라인가

Six Korean War propaganda leaflets, including 'The Mid-Autumn moon is exceptionally bright, but unfortunately I am far away in a foreign land,' no. 7194, 'They enjoy their own plays,' no. 7189, 'Under the Tyranny of Gongchang,' no. 7163, 'I'm a Veteran Communist Party member,' no. 7159, 'Ways to escape,' no. 7154, and 'Mr. White Boots delays armstice talks,' no. 7145.
