## 무슨 삐라인가

Lot of two Korean War propaganda flyers dropped over American and British soldiers encouraging them to surrender and end the war, approx. 7.5 x 5.5 inches.1. 'Use your head, soldier! If you want to keep it!', graphic on obverse shows dead soldiers on the battlefield. Verso encourages troops to not fight and surrender due to the power of the Korean military.2. 'Don't let your loved ones mourn for you!', graphic on obverse shows a mother and daughter crying. Verso reads, 'Remember your loved ones want you back home safe and sound - Why are you here, 5,000 miles from your homes, risking your lives, killing Korean men and women - even infants - who never harmed America or thought of attacking you? Why?'
