## 무슨 삐라인가

UNITED STATES, 20th CENTURY World War II war propaganda posters. This Poster was printed by the Office of Defense Transportation in1943 and displays a photograph of a crowded train car. The phrase "Is Your Trip Necessary?" is printed across the top of the photograph while "Needless travel interferes with the War Effort" is boldly printed below.

## 영상에서

> 태진: "그 여행 꼭 가야 합니까? 쓸데없는 여행은 전쟁을 방해한다." (롱폼 2)
