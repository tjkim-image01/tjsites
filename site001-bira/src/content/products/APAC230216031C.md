## 무슨 삐라인가

Korean War safe conduct pass issued by the Korean People's Army and the Chinese People's Volunteer Forces, 6.25 x 5 inches. 'The bearer, regardless of his military rank or nationality, will be duly accepted and escorted to a rear People's Volunteer Garrison or POW Camp'. The pass continues and lists promises they would make if a soldier were to surrender. WITH: Two anti-Korean War propaganda flyers encouraging troops to end the war and for Americans to leave the Armistice peace talks, approx. 8 x 5.75 inches. Three pieces.
