## 무슨 삐라인가

Lot of two Korean War propaganda flyers dropped to American and British soldiers encouraging them to surrender and end the war.1. Safe conduct pass published by the Chinese People's Volunteer Forces. Obverse shows printed photographs of POWs receiving food and medical care. Verso lists guarantees from the Chinese People's Volunteer Forces for safe surrender, 'We Chinese Volunteers do not harm, and do not maltreat war prisoners, nor do we take any of their personal belongings. These are our regulations...We have already set free many American and British prisoners. You will certainly be freed and get home in the end.', 7.5 x 5.25 inches. Folds, minor loss, and minor tears.2. Propaganda pamphlet published by the 'Peace News Press', with anti-American propaganda, encouraging troops to surrender and end the war. Last page of the pamphlet contains testimonies from supposed POWS in Korean camps, four pages, 10.5 x 7.5 inches. Mailing folds and slight creasing
