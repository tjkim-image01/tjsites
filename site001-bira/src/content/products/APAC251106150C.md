## 무슨 삐라인가

Six Korean War propaganda leaflets, including 'Homecoming,' no.8193, 'You are mice before a cat. The only way to survive is to return home,' no. 8190, 'The Communist Party is afraid you will know the truth,' no.7198, 'The coalition forces are not like the Communist forces,' no. 7238, with the English translation sheet, marked on one side in pencil, Capt. Red 8/18/59, 'A North Korean soldier receiving emergency treatment,' no. 8135, and 'Don't be a human bullet for the Communist Party,' no. 8186.
